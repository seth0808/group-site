Day 1 (emotional)

Dave and Chip pick me up from Bad Ass Coffee of Hawaii, which has moved into the space vacated by the old movie theater closing. While I don't blame them, I can't help feeling resentment toward a business that has profited from my loss.

The AC in Dave's car doesn't work and it is very hot. We crack the windows on the highway, transforming the interior into a whirling vortex where we need to scream at each other to be audible. I survive by running my sweaty neck back and forth across the airstream.

I recall a note about how people in the cattle cars would poke holes in the wood to achieve a similar aim, and think about screaming a remark, but I'm concerned that Dave will deny the Holocaust.

We stop at McDonalds for lunch. Chip lets us know that it's Burger Friday, a tradition that Chip celebrates every Friday by photographing and eating a burger. Chip shows us the channel where he celebrates Burger Friday with his Discord friends: he has dutifully uploaded a burger photo every Friday for months on end. I am overwhelmed by Chip's dedication.

We pull up to a Walmart to buy groceries for the rest of the weekend. I'm tasked to find darts. I wander around until I find an employee, who I have a strange interaction with.

Will these darts do, he says. Hm, I say. Let me speak to my dart lawyer first. What's a dart lawyer, he says. Don't worry about it, I say. It takes us two hours to leave that Walmart.

Quinn meets us at the house and we decide to go to the river together to maybe catch some fish. We would later learn that this was the wrong way to go to the river.

We discuss how saying retarded is back on the table. Retarded has its place, but never in polite company, I think. Dave says that I have the F-word pass. The F-word, I say, do you mean faggot. Dave daps me up. Boys trip has begun, Quinn says.

For dinner we want pasta, and I volunteer to make it. However, inspecting the onions, Dave says that perhaps we should cut them more finely, and, inspecting the sauce, Quinn says that perhaps we should add some pasta water. And people wonder why chefs kill themselves?

Later on, while I was pooping, the rest of the party arrives. We move some beds around (see videos) and play a game called Empire. It takes longer to explain the game than to actually play it. I come out of the game with the second to last pick but somehow end up sleeping alone in the only queen bed.

The bed has no pillow but I can't be arsed to look around for one, so I sleep on my hoodie for the rest of the trip.

Day 2 (horror)

We go to the river, taking a winding path through tall grass. The water is cold but we FUCKING JUMP IN ANYWAY (BOOM)! That cold water on a hot day feels GOOD.

Somebody catches a fish with Quinn's rod and Quinn FUCKING BASHES ITS BRAINS OUT WITH A ROCK. That fish thought its head was rock proof until it got HIT THE THIRD TIME! Quinn guts it in the river and boy, was it messy.

At some point the wind lifts Quinn's tent straight into the air like a goddamn VTOL to reveal Quinn sitting, looking around a little dazed! That is certainly some CARTOON SHIT.

No one understands how to sell foreign policy to the public, Ransom says. How do we make people CARE about Taiwan? I fucking LOVE Taiwan, Ransom whispers to me. A diamond tear rolls down his cheek. I swear I would do anything for Taiwan… I swear on my life…

I need to take a shit, Dave says.

On the way back from the river I lose my keys, but I have an Airtag on my key ring. Quinn offers to help me look for them, so we go together. I wave my phone around like a goddamn GHOST BUSTER trying to track that sweet, sweet signal.

The signal takes us deeper into the woods. The plants curl around us, the light gone wrong, the sky a distant peephole in the monstrous canopy.

It's freezing, Quinn says, I can see my breath. He's right. I stare at my phone, trying to keep my eyes open. We're right on top of the signal. I look down, down into a deep, dark ravine. At the bottom, Atharva speaks into a microphone.

Imagine this, he says. Instead of opening one door, imagine if we opened millions of them…

Day 3 (Tropic Thunder)

We go hiking.

On the trail, we find the smoldering remains of a campfire. Several of us take turns pissing on it. Seth adjusts his bullet proof vest. Can't be too careful, he says.

The unit comes to a raging river. Let's split up, someone says. We pace up and down the bank, looking for an easy crossing.

It's quiet. Too quiet.

Shots ring out from the treeline.

CHARLIE!

Dave goes down hard, losing a sock. Chip returns fire. I fumble for my radio, keying in coordinates. The treeline evaporates into a plume of napalm. I throw my shoes across the water and make my way across.

We confirm the kills and keep moving. Dave wipes his nose. God damn it, he says. I lost my sock.

At the top of the ridge, we connect with Falcon Platoon. Lion's Head is close, they say. Through there? No, no. Maybe there? No. Home for Christmas, Seth said. Full of shit.

We make it to the top of the mountain. It's beautiful. We eat our packed sandwiches. Dave counts the Pringles as I eat them, one by one. I'm gonna get my fair share, he says. My second sandwich is in the fridge.

On the other side of the mountain, we're ambushed again. The gunfire is deafening. Sawyer pulls me into his foxhole. What would you be doing if you weren't here? I don't know, I say. I've always wanted to set up an import/export business. He makes a face. That's so interesting, can you tell me more?

A grenade whistles through the foxhole opening and clatters on the floor. Atharva chuckles. That's the thing about escaping a local maximum. You have to take a step backward to go two steps forward. He curls around the grenade, smiling. I can't look away.
