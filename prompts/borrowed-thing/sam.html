My Mother's Voice

by Sam

I borrowed my mother's way of saying "sweetheart" — the long ee, the emphasis on the second syllable, the slight exhale before it. I don't know when I took it. Sometime between eighteen and thirty, it moved into my mouth and stayed.

She noticed first. We were on the phone and I said it to my dog, loud enough to carry, and there was a pause on her end. That's mine, she said. She wasn't wrong.

I've never given it back. I suspect she doesn't want me to.
